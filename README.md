# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Microsoft Excel. I developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard to track key business metrics, analyze revenue and profitability, and understand sales performance across different countries, regions, product categories, and sales channels.

The dashboard helps transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Monitor gross profit and gross profit margin.
- Identify top-performing countries based on revenue.
- Compare monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Understand revenue contribution across different sales channels.
- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Provide interactive filtering for better decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Excel Formulas
- Interactive Dashboard
- Map Chart

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following business metrics:

- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Revenue & Profit Analysis
Visualizes monthly revenue and gross profit trends to identify sales patterns and profitability changes.

### 2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
Highlights the top five countries contributing the highest revenue.

### 3. Revenue Share by Channel
Analyzes revenue distribution across:
- Corporate
- Marketplace
- Online Store
- Retail Partners

### 4. Region-wise Comparison
Compares sales performance across different geographical regions.

### 5. Country-wise Map Chart
Provides a geographical view of sales performance across countries.

### 6. Interactive Slicers
The dashboard includes interactive filters for:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter

These filters allow users to explore sales performance dynamically.


## 💡 Key Business Insights

The dashboard enables business users to:

- Identify high-revenue countries and regions.
- Track monthly revenue and profitability trends.
- Understand which sales channels contribute the most revenue.
- Compare gross profit against total revenue.
- Evaluate sales performance across different quarters and product categories.
